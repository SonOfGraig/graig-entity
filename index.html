02 · How the merge works

From remembering him to making him better.

Each stage has to earn the next. It's built in public, so the status of each one is shown honestly.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Live

    Conversations, voice notes and decisions go into a permanent, encrypted memory, backed up off the machine.

  2. 02

    Model

    Live

    Facts about the world are kept apart from the model of Tumiso himself, in the layers of his memory. His most personal facts never reach an AI provider.

  3. 03

    Predict

    Building

    Graig predicts Tumiso's choices before he makes them, then scores himself against what Tumiso really did.

  4. 04

    Strengthen

    Next

    Weak spots are found only from evidence and confirmed by Tumiso, then turned into drills and "you first" prompts.

05 · What stays private

A twin of a person is the most personal data there is.

So it's treated that way: sealed, gated, visible to its owner, and curated before anything goes public.

  1. Seal 01open

    Encrypted at rest

    The model of Tumiso is sealed on hardware he owns. His most personal facts are kept out of every model call.

  2. Seal 02open

    A privacy gate on every call

    Private conversation only goes to providers that don't keep or train on it. Keys and card numbers are scrubbed before anything leaves the machine.

  3. Seal 03open

    Nothing runs unseen

    Every capability Graig has appears in his console. Anything that writes without leaving a record gets flagged.

  4. Seal 04open

    Public means curated

    This page only shows work Tumiso has approved. It never mirrors Graig's private log or his newest post automatically.
